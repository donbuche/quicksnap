Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short

  • The QuickSnap app collects nothing: no accounts, no uploads, no telemetry.
  • This website only uses Google Analytics if you accept it. Until then, nothing is sent to Google.
  • You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings button below or in the footer of any page.

Who is responsible

The QuickSnap website at https://donbuche.github.io/quicksnap/, in all its languages, is run by Joan Galtés i Moreno, Catalonia (Spain), who is the data controller for the analytics described here.

To ask anything about your data or to exercise your rights, write to donbuche@gmail.com.

The QuickSnap app

The app runs entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts and does not send your screenshots, recordings or any usage data anywhere. Your settings are stored on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/QuickSnap/.

Google Analytics on this website

If you accept, the website loads Google Analytics 4 to learn how many people visit, in which languages, and how many download the app. It records:

  • the pages you view, the page you came from, and the language of the page;
  • clicks on the download buttons (which button, on which language page) and on the language links;
  • your approximate location (country and city), worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses;
  • general information about your device and browser: type, operating system, screen size and browser language.

Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, so the data is not linked to your Google account or used for advertising. The data is kept for 14 months and then deleted.

Legal basis: your consent (article 6.1.a of the GDPR, and article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE for cookies). You can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it.

Who processes it: Google Ireland Limited, on behalf of the website. Google may transfer data to the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and how Google Analytics safeguards data.

Hosting and downloads

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app is downloaded from GitHub Releases. GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address when you visit or download, and keeps it in its logs for security. This happens with or without analytics; see the GitHub privacy statement.

The website loads no other external resources: no web fonts, no social media buttons, no advertising.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Analytics data is pseudonymous: it cannot be linked to you by name. The quickest way to stop it and remove it from your browser is to reject analytics in the cookie settings.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes to this policy

If the website starts collecting anything new, this page will be updated first and you will be asked again before it applies to you. The date at the top shows the last change.
